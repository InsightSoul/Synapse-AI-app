# Synapse AI Membership Pricing Draft

Status: planning draft. Prices, quotas, taxes, and billing provider are not set.

## Membership tiers

| Tier | Positioning | Proposed access |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Limited, no-cost introduction | Small monthly text-analysis allowance; short history retention; no saved raw audio by default; standard processing. Exact limits TBD. |
| Standard | Affordable everyday membership | Larger monthly text allowance; a modest audio-transcription allowance; full access to the four supported languages; saved analysis history. Price and limits TBD. |
| Premium | Higher-priced, full-feature membership | Highest service priority; generous analysis and transcription allowances; extended history and recording storage controls; advanced exports and profile tools as they are built. Price and limits TBD. |

## Payment options

- One-time purchase: a single analysis or a time-limited credit pack; no recurring charge. Define credit quantity and expiry before launch.
- Monthly membership: recurring Standard or Premium billing, cancelable by the customer. Show renewal date and cancellation terms before purchase.
- Unlimited membership: a recurring Premium option with unlimited use only if provider costs and abuse controls make that sustainable. Otherwise describe it as a generous fair-use allowance, not literally unlimited.

## Recommended launch structure

1. Keep the Free tier as a limited trial of text analysis.
2. Offer Standard as the low-cost monthly option and optionally a one-time credit pack.
3. Offer Premium as the higher-priced monthly option. Add an unlimited label only after cost monitoring and fair-use terms are defined.

## Decisions to make before implementation

- Currency and displayed tax treatment.
- Exact text-analysis, audio-transcription, and storage quotas for each tier.
- Whether one-time purchases expire and whether unused credits roll over.
- Whether Premium is truly unlimited, fair-use limited, or quota based.
- Refund, cancellation, renewal, and failed-payment policies.
- Billing provider, invoicing requirements, and account downgrade behavior.
- Whether saved recordings have a plan-specific retention limit.

Do not present any plan as unlimited until the included third-party AI and transcription costs have been measured and the fair-use policy is published.
